# Talent Bridge Global Solutions — Brand Guidelines and Production Specifications

## 1. Visual Identity and Emblem Architecture

The Talent Bridge Global Solutions brand identity embodies executive credibility, pan-African human capital empowerment, and international cross-border connectivity.

### Symbolism Breakdown
- **Upper Arch (Growth Green - `#2EA44F`):** Represents sunrise, renewal, continuous learning, and flourishing human potential.
- **Lower Foundation Arch (Deep Corporate Navy - `#0F2B48`):** Signifies legal rigor, fiduciary stability, institutional governance, and compliance.
- **Global Coordinate Sphere (Horizon Blue - `#3E7CB1` / Pale Ice - `#E8F2FA`):** Represents worldwide connectivity, positioning African professionals on global stages. The sphere features clean coordinate graticules without local color patches.
- **Arched Suspension Bridge:** Bridges human capital with commercial opportunity, connecting local talent to regional and international markets.
- **The Three Central Figures:** Represents collaborative executive partnership. The central figure is colored in Growth Green (`#2EA44F`), flanked by two figures in Deep Corporate Navy (`#0F2B48`).
- **North Star Accent (Warm Gold - `#C9A45C`):** Denotes clarity of direction, ethical governance, and professional excellence.

## 3. Typography Standards

- **Primary Display and Headings:** `Outfit` (Bold 700 / ExtraBold 800).
- **Body, Contracts and Handbooks:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600).
- **Editorial and Legal Text:** `Newsreader` (Editorial Serif for governance and board-level documentation).

## 4. Print and Collateral Specifications

### Tri-Fold Brochure (6 Panels)
- **Flat Size:** Standard A4 (297 mm × 210 mm) Landscape.
- **Folded Size:** 99 mm × 210 mm.
- **Recommended Stock:** 250gsm to 300gsm Silk / Matte Coated Art Paper.
- **Finishing:** Matte dispersion varnish or soft-touch lamination on outer spread. Creased before folding to prevent spine cracking.

### Exhibition Roll-Up Banner
- **Finished Size:** 850 mm × 2000 mm.
- **Material:** 440gsm anti-curl polyester blockout film.
- **Printing Method:** UV cured or eco-solvent high-definition wide-format digital print.

### A4 Capability One-Pager
- **Finished Size:** 210 mm × 297 mm Portrait.
- **Recommended Stock:** 170gsm to 200gsm Silk Text or Smooth Uncoated Premium Bond.

### Executive Business Cards
- **Finished Size:** Standard 3.5" × 2.0" (88.9 mm × 50.8 mm).
- **Bleed:** 3 mm around all borders.
- **Recommended Stock:** 350gsm Heavyweight Linen or Matte Velvet Touch cardstock.
